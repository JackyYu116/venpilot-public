What does VenPilot provide?

VenPilot develops cashless payment systems, IoT hardware integration and management software for unattended and self-service retail. Our focus is the connected system behind your business: how customers pay, how equipment connects and how your team manages operations.

How can VenPilot help my business grow?

Our approach centers on three goals: reduce operating effort, make more purchases easier to complete and help customers return. We connect payment, device and business information so you can work more efficiently and make better commercial decisions.

Are these solutions limited to the industries shown?

No. The industry examples are starting points, with many different applications within each. If your business relies on unattended payments or self-service experiences, tell us about your workflow and integration needs.

Can VenPilot connect to equipment and software I already use?

Our API-first approach starts with your existing machine interfaces, payment requirements and business software. Share those details so we can assess the right integration for your operating environment.

Which payment terminal is featured?

PAX IM30 is the featured all-in-one unattended payment terminal. It brings Android-based interaction, cashless payment acceptance, camera and scanning functions, and machine interfaces into an embedded design. See the IM30 page for capabilities and configuration details.

How does the platform approach AI and data?

VenPilot emphasizes an AI-ready data foundation: bringing payment, device and customer information together for analysis. This creates a clearer starting point for AI-assisted insight into sales, operating efficiency and repeat purchases. The analytics workflow is scoped to each integration.
