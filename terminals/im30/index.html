IM30 AT A GLANCE

A practical foundation for your next integration.

Operating platform
Android-based smart platform for customizable applications and workflows
Payment acceptance
Chip & PIN, magnetic stripe, contactless / NFC, QR code and mobile wallets

IP65 ingress protection and IK08 impact resistance on supported configurations
Data capture
